# A7 v5.3 Eight-Camera Construction Design

## Goal

Eliminate the view-specific lower visibility failures by constructing the
candidate from renderer evidence covering both the original evidence cameras
and the already-consumed CoreView377 validation cameras.

## Diagnosis

v5.2 fixed the original c22 failure but moved normalized target-response risk to
c17, c18, c20, c21, and c23. The optimizer already enforces normalized
visibility per construction camera; the failure occurred because construction
contained only c01/c05/c09/c13. Tightening that four-camera threshold again
would not constrain the unseen target phase.

## Selected Approach

Generate a new renderer-aligned dual evidence artifact over:

```text
c01 c05 c09 c13 c17 c18 c19 c20
frames 0:570:5
```

Run the existing fold-local support, v4 lower seed, target, soft-IoU, and
normalized visibility constraints over all eight cameras. The formal
eight-camera feasibility sweep fixes construction visibility at `<=0.9980`
and construction target response at `>=0.995`; held-out visibility remains
`<=1.0` and retrospective target response remains `>=0.99`.

The full construction candidate must improve outer and boundary by at least
0.5%. A single LOCO held-out camera is a direction and constraint check rather
than a second aggregate-effect-size test: its outer and boundary ratios must
both be below 1.0 while target, visibility, and soft-IoU constraints pass.

This is preferable to another threshold-only repair because it constrains the
actual camera phases that failed. It is preferable to expanding the action grid
because v5.2 already proved that equal-capacity carrier replacement preserves
outer/boundary and spatial quality.

## Frozen Method

- Hair remains exact A5 unless the existing temporal-shortfall rule opens at
  most three moves.
- Lower starts from v4.
- Coordinate actions remain 5%/10%.
- Maximum lower changed fraction remains 20%.
- Topology, coverage, frozen parts, and weight `<=A5` remain exact.
- Evidence soft-IoU drop remains at most 0.005.
- Spatial and retrospective gates remain unchanged.

Carrier IDs are generated by the optimizer and are not hard-coded.

## Evidence Freeze

Use a dedicated v5.3 evidence contract to avoid a circular dependency between
the evidence contract fingerprint and the evidence file SHA-256. After the
eight-camera artifact is generated, freeze a separate v5.3 candidate contract
containing the evidence SHA-256 and source contract fingerprint.

## Evaluation Integrity

c17-c20 are now development construction cameras and can no longer be described
as validation. c21-c23 remain retrospective because they were opened in v5.1.
The CoreView377 run therefore remains `paper_test_eligible=false`. A successful
v5.3 run only freezes the algorithm for clean five-subject LOSO testing.

## Development Gates

- Eight-camera LOCO: 8/8 folds pass.
- Full construction candidate: outer and boundary improve at least 0.5%, every
  construction camera has visibility at most 0.9980 and target response at
  least 0.995.
- Each LOCO held-out camera improves both temporal metrics directionally and
  passes its target, visibility, and soft-IoU constraints.
- Retrospective c21-c23: both temporal metrics improve, visibility is at most
  1.0, and target response is at least 0.99.
- Spatial burden, coverage, soft-IoU, macro/micro IoU, topology, provenance, and
  bank identity pass unchanged thresholds.
